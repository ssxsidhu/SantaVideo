One continuous take of Santa Claus in his cozy log cabin by the fire. At the start he is quietly reading a child's crayon letter. On "Oh!" he looks up from the letter, eyebrows lifting in delighted surprise, and laughs warmly as he lowers the letter out of frame. Then he talks gently and kindly straight to the camera, with small natural head movements and expressive eyes. Near the end he gives a small friendly wave. Camera is static, no zoom, no push-in. Background stays still except the fire flickering and the tree lights twinkling. Photorealistic, warm firelight.
