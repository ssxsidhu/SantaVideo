Santa Claus talks warmly and gently straight into the camera lens, keeping eye contact with the viewer the whole time, like a kind grandfather on a video call. At the very start he is pleasantly surprised to see the viewer: eyebrows lift and he gives one soft, warm chuckle. After that he is calm and sincere: a gentle smile, relaxed face, mouth moving only to speak, no big open-mouthed laughing. Eyes stay open and clearly visible through his glasses. Small natural head movements. His hands stay low and still; he does not wave or gesture. Camera is static and locked off: no zoom, no push-in, no pull-out. Background stays still except the fire flickering softly. Photorealistic, warm firelight.
