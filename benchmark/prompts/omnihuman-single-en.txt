One continuous take of Santa Claus in his cozy log cabin by the fire, holding a child's crayon letter. He has just looked up from reading it. On his first word he is delighted and surprised to see the viewer: eyebrows up, eyes wide, a big happy laugh. He lowers the letter to his lap and keeps it there. He talks warmly and kindly straight into the camera, keeping eye contact with the viewer, eyes open and clearly visible through his glasses, with small natural head movements. Hands stay low; he does not wave. Camera is static and locked off: no zoom, no push-in, no pull-out. Background stays still except the fire flickering and the tree lights twinkling. Photorealistic, warm firelight.
